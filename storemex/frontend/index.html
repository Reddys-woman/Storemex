About Storemex

The story, the mission, and the humans behind it.

Why we built this

We take a pantry item, save a expiry date, or download a receipt a dozen times a day — and watch groceries expire before you use them. Storemex exists because organizing that mess shouldn't be another chore on your list. It should just happen, quietly, in the background, so the only thing left for you to do is remember that you saved something — Storemex handles the rest.

We're a small team obsessed with making "where did I save that?" a question you never have to ask again.

Built by the Storemex team

Storemex is designed and developed by the Storemex team, a small team building focused, AI-native tools for everyday information overload.

Our Product

Restock happens smarter. You just cook.

Smart Search

Find anything in your pantry instantly — by name, category, or status like “expiring,” “low stock,” or “unavailable.” No digging through lists.

Restock & Expiry Reminders

Get alerts when items are running low or nearing expiry — so you restock in time and waste less food without checking the fridge every day.

Alexa Integration

Ask Alexa what’s left, what’s expiring, or what to add to the shopping list — hands-free pantry updates while you cook.

Webcam Gestures

Swipe through your shopping list with simple hand gestures on camera — add to cart or save for later without touching the keyboard.

Expiry from Images

Snap a product label or receipt and Storemex extracts the expiry date for you — no manual typing, just point and save.

History & Logs

Every add, update, and removal is logged in a clear timeline — so you always know what changed in your pantry and when.
